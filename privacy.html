Privacy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Signing in with Google, Microsoft or Zoho

When you connect your account to an Infinity computer, Infinity asks that service only for your name, e-mail address and profile picture (the "openid", "email" and "profile" permissions). It never sees your password: you type it on the service's own page.

  • They are kept only on your computer, to show who is signed in and to check that it's you when you choose a new PIN.
  • Infinity has no server that stores them, and it never sells or shares them.
  • Infinity does not read your mail, files, contacts or anything else in that account.
Forgot your PIN?

If you ask for a link by e-mail, the e-mail is sent through Google Firebase to the address of your account. Firebase keeps that address to send the link.

Stop using it

In Settings, Accounts, choose "Use a local account instead": your computer forgets the account. You can also remove Infinity's access from your Google, Microsoft or Zoho account settings at any time.

Questions

Use the support address shown on the Google sign-in page for Infinity.
